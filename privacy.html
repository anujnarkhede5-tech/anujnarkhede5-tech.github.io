Privacy Policy for Overnote

Effective Date: 1 May 2026

Last updated: 1 May 2026

Overnote ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide

  • Notes content (text, drawings, or media you create)
  • Account details (such as email, if you sign in)

b) Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (device type, OS version)
  • App usage data (features used, interactions)
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Save and display your notes
  • Sync your data across devices (if enabled)
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Provide support and updates

3. Third-Party Services

Overnote may use trusted third-party services, including:

Google Firebase (Authentication, Database, Analytics, Crash Reporting)

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Your notes and data may be stored securely on cloud servers (if sync is enabled)
  • We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access
  • However, no system is completely secure

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except:

  • To provide core app functionality (e.g., cloud storage)
  • When required by law

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your notes or account
  • Stop using the app at any time

7. Children's Privacy

Overnote is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Permissions

The app may request permissions such as:

  • Storage (to save notes)
  • Overlay (to display pinned notes)
  • Notifications (for reminders)

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the app or on this page.
